# 3D Visual Discovery Library Specification

The PromptDock 7.0 visual catalog contains 30 production-tested 3D website visual effects across 8 distinct categories.

## Categories & Visual Index

### 1. 3D Hero Experiences
- **Aurora Plasma 3D Hero**: Procedural glowing 3D plasma mesh with sine wave vertex displacement.
- **Celestial Orbit Planet**: Interactive low-poly celestial sphere with Keplerian satellite orbits.
- **Kinetic Abstract Torus Sculpture**: Intertwined mathematical torus knots executing gyroscopic rotation.
- **Spatial Glassmorphism Prismatic Hero**: Translucent optical glass planes with physical refraction.
- **Kinetic Typography 3D Marquee**: Dynamic 3D letterforms projecting along cylindrical curves.

### 2. 3D Backgrounds
- **Particle Galaxy Vortex**: 30,000-particle logarithmic spiral galaxy with curl-noise dynamics.
- **Animated Starfield Warp**: Relativistic depth starfield accelerating on scroll velocity.
- **Neural Synthesis Network**: Interconnected 3D synaptic node mesh with traversing pulse signals.
- **Procedural Low-Poly Terrain**: Perlin-noise heightmap terrain flying forward with wireframe facet lighting.

### 3. Particles & Physics
- **Mouse-Reactive Particle Dispersion**: Force-field particle array that repels from cursor with spring physics.
- **Magnetic Physics Attractor Field**: Gravitational nodes orbiting dynamic draggable attractors.
- **GPU Curl-Noise Particle Vortex**: Vector curl-noise advection rendering 40k instanced sparkles.
- **Dynamic Vector Flow Field 3D**: Continuous streamlines flowing through Perlin potential gradients.

### 4. 3D Product Experiences
- **Spatial Headphone 3D Showcase**: 360-degree turntable with exploded planar driver view and hotspots.
- **Luxury Chrono Watch Turntable**: High-precision mechanical watch tourbillon with anisotropic steel.
- **Spatial Hardware Terminal Gallery**: Retro-futuristic holographic console with interactive scanlines.

### 5. Motion and Scroll Experiences
- **Scroll-Driven 3D Storytelling Camera**: Spline camera journey swooping through spatial waypoints.
- **Spatial Multi-Plane Parallax Scene**: Multi-layered depth composition with inertial lerping.
- **Cinematic Section 3D Dissolve**: Procedural geometry slicing and Voronoi dissolutions between sections.

### 6. 3D UI Components
- **Spatial 3D Specular Tilt Cards**: UI cards with real 3D perspective pitch/roll and specular glare.
- **Morphing 3D Dynamic Island Nav**: Compact pill navbar expanding into a 3D media tray.
- **Magnetic 3D Glow Specular Button**: CTA button physically attracted to cursor coordinates.
- **Border Beam SVG Keyframe Runner**: Glowing light runner racing along card perimeters.

### 7. Shaders and Materials
- **WebGL Liquid Chrome Fluid Shader**: Fragment shader simulating viscous liquid mercury with cursor ripples.
- **Physical Glass Optical Refraction**: Shader calculating real-time Fresnel reflection and chromatic dispersion.
- **Gooey Metamorphic Fluid Spheres**: Metaball fluid clusters smoothly merging with surface tension.

### 8. AI and Technology Visuals
- **AI Holographic Thinking Energy Orb**: Ethereal orb with swirling volumetric energy strands.
- **Multi-Agent Neural Synthesis Graph**: Interactive 3D force-directed graph depicting agent messages.
- **Real-time FFT Voice Audio Equalizer**: Circular frequency equalizer bar ring reacting to voice input.
- **Spatial Telemetry Observability Matrix**: High-density 3D data grid with glowing sparkline cylinders.
